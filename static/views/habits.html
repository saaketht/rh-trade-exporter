Oil thesis, tested 2026-09-11 (CL=F vs SPY, 5-min Aug 24–Sep 10 and 1-min Sep 2–10): the inverse oil/SPY move is real and was strongest on Sep 8–10 (same-bar correlation −0.53 / −0.65 / −0.85), but it is coincident, not leading — every lagged correlation is ≈0 at both resolutions. A mechanical "oil stalled after rising" rule had no forward SPY edge (51% hit overall, 39% during the streak), oil state at entry had zero correlation with decision P/L, and the losing week Aug 31–Sep 4 saw the same oil states. Oil explains the regime; the behaviors above explain the P/L. Treat it as context, not a trigger.
